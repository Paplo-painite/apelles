Apelles app icon (apelles.svg, apelles-small.svg and every file derived from them in
this directory: apelles-1024.png, apelles.icns, apelles.ico, hicolor/).

The fox artwork is a modified derivative of the original PhotoCraft project icon. It was cropped
and placed on a red background for Apelles. The original PhotoCraft icon was released by its
project owner under either of

  * Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  * MIT license (https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

at your option.

The Apelles crop, layout and background modifications are released under the same project license.

Regenerate the derived files with packaging/icons.sh.
